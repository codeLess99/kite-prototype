Fixing Provider Data Before It Breaks

Kite spots a likely change, asks the office one pre-filled question, checks the answer with R3, and pushes it into plan systems in under 48 hours. No new login. No re-typing.

1. Detect

Claims, NPPES and licence signals show a likely change (Directory Accuracy monitoring).

2. Ask once

Office gets one plan-branded magic link. No login, pre-filled from HiPr.

3. Validate

R3 scores the reply instantly. Plain-language fix if something's wrong.

4. Close the loop

Above threshold → auto-approved to FACETS/QNXT in <48h. Removals always get a human.
